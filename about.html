About

Too many good decisions get made on bad numbers.

Almost every business we meet has the same problem underneath the obvious one: nobody quite agrees on the number, so nobody quite trusts the decision. AI is now sitting on top of that, sounding certain. Closing that gap is the whole reason this firm exists.

From the founder

Why I started this

I've spent over a decade doing this work from the inside. I ran the Data Innovation Lab at Tech Impact, where we built data and AI capacity for nonprofits. Before that I was lead data scientist at CompassRed, doing the same for businesses. Somewhere in the middle I started Open Data Delaware, which spent five years arguing that public data should be understandable by the people it's actually about.

The pattern never changed. The technology was rarely the problem. What was missing was somebody willing to settle what a customer is, when revenue counts, and which date wins when two systems disagree — then write it down where everyone could see it. That work isn't glamorous and it's usually nobody's job.

Then AI made it urgent. A model pointed at unsettled numbers doesn't hesitate — it answers fluently, and now the wrong figure arrives with a paragraph of reasoning attached. Most of the AI projects we get called about are data projects wearing a different hat.

So that's the order here: get the numbers straight, then let AI earn its keep on top of them. I'd rather tell you that on the first call than three months in.
